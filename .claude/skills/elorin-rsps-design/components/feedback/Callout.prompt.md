One-line: pulls one fact out of the prose - a shortcut (tip), a caveat (note), a costly mistake (warning), or something that will get you killed (danger).

```jsx
<Callout tone="danger" title="Hardcore">
  Dying here ends the run. Bank your gear before the second phase.
</Callout>
```

- Left accent bar + icon + uppercase mono title; body copy is secondary grey.
- Two callouts in a row means the prose is doing its job badly. Pick one.
