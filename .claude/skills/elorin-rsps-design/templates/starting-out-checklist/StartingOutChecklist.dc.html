Starting out

Work down the list. Tick things off as you go. Progress is saved on this device, so nobody sees your list but you. Nothing here is timed and nothing is missable.

Tip

Bank at Edgeville. The Grand Exchange clone, the fairy ring and the Slayer master are all one click from the bank chest.
[ Step 01 ]

First hour

0/4

Get off Tutorial Island and into the game proper.

Skip it if you have played before. You keep the starter kit either way.

Do it before your first trip out of Edgeville.

  • Claim points at the Vote shop
  • Buy a Vote mystery box
Easy

Home teleport first, then the ::train and ::shops commands.

Coming from OSRS?[ Optional ]

No EOC, no OSRS accuracy formulas. Prayer switching matters more, tick eating matters less. Drop tables follow the revision each boss came from, so read the table on the boss page rather than the OSRS wiki.
